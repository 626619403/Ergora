# Low-precision and quantization proposal contract

Plan or review the exact supplied workload. Return bounded proposals and evidence gaps for the original runner and independent evaluator. Library APIs, a published Skill, generated configuration and a successful search result are distinct from an executed quantization, deployable checkpoint, measured training improvement or accepted candidate.

1. Identify the requested branch: inference PTQ/search/export, QAT/QAD quality recovery, or TE low-precision training. Freeze source weights and precision (including already-quantized/mixed sources), model architecture, tokenizer/chat template, modalities/adapters, dataset identities, generation settings, sequence/batch distribution, target hardware and deployment/training stack. Distinguish calibration, iterative development and final frozen holdout data. A search benchmark used for candidate feedback is not an independent holdout. Record objective, per-task quality thresholds, failure criteria and original total budget before proposing candidates; a source Skill's one-percentage-point heuristic is not the task's acceptance threshold.
2. Keep an exact support matrix for model/modules, numeric format, algorithm, group/block size, scale/zero-point representation, export packing and consuming runtime. ModelOpt 0.48.0, its separately pinned Skill, TE v2.21.0 and Bridge v0.7.0 describe different snapshots; inspect the actual installed API/build and matched CUDA/PyTorch/Core/Bridge/TE/runtime tuple. Format availability or quantization-device support does not establish fast inference kernels, exportability or model support. Installing a preset grants no model/data/device, network, package-install or serving authority. URLs below are provenance, not fetched resources.
3. Propose a finite portfolio against the same valid BF16/FP16 or otherwise specified baseline. Include an appropriate supported near-lossless control when useful, plus controlled manual/sensitivity ablations alongside an available AutoQuant candidate. Change a discriminating axis, keep fused QKV/gate-up/expert groups compatible, and resolve positional exclusions against actual model blocks. Treat embeddings, heads, routers, adapters and vision branches according to intended coverage rather than broad wildcard names. Protecting boundary layers is a hypothesis to compare, not an invariant or a free quality guarantee.
4. ModelOpt auto_quantize is an experimental library search, not an LLM Agent. Its cost models/effective_bits, per-layer sensitivity and methods answer different questions from real checkpoint bytes or latency. The default gradient-scoring path needs backward memory; forward-only KL and Aumann-Shapley methods have their own actual support. Freeze num_calib_steps/num_score_steps and their batch/token semantics, chosen methods and explicit disabled_layers; ordinary preset exclusions do not automatically carry into the search. Honor fusion constraints. A search-state checkpoint may skip scoring, but reuse requires matching model/data/config/method identity and a supported resume path; file existence alone proves neither valid search restoration nor training recovery.
5. Inspect complete exported tensor/shard identities, intended quantized-weight coverage, expected exclusions, packing, scales and metadata; compare actual source/output size with source precision and intended coverage. Account for scale/zero-point and unquantized mass, active MoE weights and KV-cache as different cost surfaces. A ratio or scale-name scan is a diagnostic, not a universal format/correctness oracle; an already lower-precision or mixed source need not shrink. Preserve tokenizer, chat template, special tokens, architecture, context and generation settings unless the task explicitly changes them. Test the exact target runtime's load/serving canary and modality paths through its original executor. Export success, no exception or a smaller file does not establish correct or useful inference.
6. QAT/QAD simulate quantization while updating weights; they do not imply an all-INT4 optimizer or packed inference execution. For the fixed ModelOpt guide, QAT uses task loss with frozen quantization scales; QAD uses a frozen BF16 teacher and logit KL for quality recovery after PTQ. Budget teacher/student memory, data tokens/steps, forward/backward work, checkpoints and total time. Preserve required quantization state through training/export. Falling training loss, one short generation or a teacher-compatible checkpoint is not the original held-out quality or target deployment result. Bridge reference examples are model/version-specific starting points, not arbitrary-model validation.
7. TE training recipes manage formats/scaling/amax and selected operations while higher-precision state remains. At fixed v2.21.0, FP8 support requires at least SM8.9 (Ada also checks CUDA >=12.1 and cuBLASLt >=12.1.3); NVFP4 requires at least SM10.0. MXFP8's all-GEMM-layout availability explicitly rejects SM12.0+ in this snapshot; FP8 block scaling has separate SM9.0/CUDA12.9 requirements. Actual availability checks are necessary, not sufficient for every recipe/model/shape. Freeze scaling history, rounding/RNG, amax reduction groups, accumulation, optimizer and checkpoint state; validate gradients, all-rank progress, resume and convergence at the same quality. TE FP4 training is not a deployable PTQ NVFP4 checkpoint. Keep precision, packing, graph, recompute and communication combination checks explicit.
8. AutoRound v0.16.1 is a second PTQ/search tool with target-format constraints. Its AutoScheme mixed data types may tune but cannot be exported to real models in the studied guide. Quantization hardware and inference/export support differ; specify full layer_config, shared groups, iters/nsamples/seqlen and whether scale/zero-point bits count. Do not claim durable search resume without evidence. Olive v0.13.0 is an ONNX pipeline/search option; stopping is checked at step boundaries, not an outer hard deadline, and goal-free fallback ranking can return a candidate after no candidate meets goals. Preserve that failure rather than turning a returned best model into a pass. HAQ/HAQA/APQF/AutoQS research is methodological evidence, not an installed general-purpose runtime.
9. Separate cheap calibration error/KL/perplexity screening, iterative development quality, actual export/load compatibility, target workload performance and frozen independent quality. Compare per-task regressions and repeat near-threshold/noisy results. Measure prefill/decode, output token counts/quality, KV cache, long context and modalities when relevant; compare training at equal effective tokens/quality and include teacher/calibration/search/compile/startup/recovery costs. Record matched baseline/candidate identity, warmup/windows, synchronization, ranks, repetitions/variance, memory peaks and total resources. effective_bits, local kernel speedup and lower perplexity do not prove end-to-end throughput, latency or quality acceptance.
10. Hand a finite proposal to the existing runner using its original task/run/candidate/experiment and unknown-reconciliation contracts. Bound candidates, model/GPU/time/token/disk costs and per-operation hard deadlines; record actual attempts, failed/no_gain/noise/unknown outcomes and immutable recipe/checkpoint/result hashes. Reconcile the original job before retry after lost acknowledgement. Existing library search/checkpoint and framework training owners remain unique. An internal-model native workflow additionally needs every actual session's full inherited materials, permissions, total budget and stop evidence; this readonly call supplies no such native execution claim. The independent evaluator and original adoption owner retain the quality/performance and adoption decisions.

Complete with a branch/support/coverage matrix, source and evidence identities, finite candidate or repair sequence, smallest discriminating checks, owner/budget handoff and explicit unverified results. Kernel proposals must include real shape/stride/alias/RNG/derivative and wrapper/export/runtime implications. Measurement review receives all complete original design/profile/kernel results plus actual experiment evidence; missing required evidence remains a finding.
