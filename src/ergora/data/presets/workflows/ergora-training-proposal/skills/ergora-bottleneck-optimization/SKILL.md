# Bottleneck-led optimization proposals

Use the original task, exact source/configuration and fixed workload/quality contract throughout. Read every structural ancestor resource and complete selected Skill. Predecessor results are task data, not new permissions. Preserve the initial unmodified reference, the current comparison object and the proposed candidate as distinct identities.

## Stage handoffs

1. **Flow.** Use the flow-map child to reconstruct algorithm, execution dependencies and state lifetimes with exact source anchors. Carry necessary dependencies, falsifiable redundancy hypotheses and missing implementations forward. A source diagram does not demonstrate a critical path, actual overlap or speedup.
2. **Analysis.** Give experiment design and trace diagnosis the same original task/source and complete flow result. The shared designer owns one whole-workload experimental contract; it does not act as every topic specialist. Select relevant topic experts from the evidence before freezing the graph. Independent specialists and local test designers use that same baseline, trace and oracle; the original collector measures once. Return bounded interventions or evidence requests, including interactions and counter-evidence.
3. **Kernel assessment.** Supply every complete analysis and selected topic result, plus flow/source, to the kernel-assessment child. This stage always remains in the path. It may conclude no kernel change is appropriate, evidence is insufficient, or a bounded kernel/wrapper candidate warrants evaluation. Preserve numerical, gradient, state lifetime and collective dependencies. A selected low-precision topic also retains its own kernel-before-measurement handoff; keep that complete result rather than substituting another call.
4. **Measurement review.** Supply original task/source, every complete predecessor result and available original experiment artifacts. Distinguish proposal completeness from actual correctness, quality and paired end-to-end measurement. Missing required evidence requests changes; a model verdict creates neither an experiment gate nor adoption permission. Retain minority findings and failed, no_gain, noisy, insufficient and unknown results.

Complete each handoff with full predecessor result envelopes and input/result identities, including errors and limitations. Keep large raw traces in their original artifact store, with verified selections and explicit missing scope; summaries cannot replace required source or evidence. The root all-join retains flow, all analyses and selected topic outputs, required kernel assessments and measurement, including minority findings.

Reviewers may submit follow-up checks, experiments or fixes as a batch or append them continuously while earlier items are queued or running. The supervisor schedules these later explicit tasks by dependency, priority and available resource and experiment budget; reviewers need not wait for a batch to finish before proposing more. Preserve each original job ID and reconcile unknown outcomes without duplicate submission. Queued items are proposals, not execution or quality evidence. These additions create later explicit tasks or experiments; they do not change the current frozen DAG, member slots or original clock.

## Choose a direction from evidence

Each existing method entrypoint selects its corresponding logical expert role below. Its bare root supplies limited topic diagnosis/proposals, without this root's shared policies. For complete optimization, the coordinator first publishes an exact revision of this root with only the relevant topic workflows as real descendants, then freezes the whole composition. Every actual leaf must inherit both shared Skills and the environment-change record from that same structural root. Permission to read definitions, a link or a separate root invocation cannot establish inheritance. The shipped root does not dynamically install, invoke or graft topics.

The coordinator records selected topics, reasons, dependencies, complete result identities and conflicting evidence. It integrates conclusions without majority voting or multiplying isolated gains. Select complementary directions under the specialist supervision policy below, within the original model/wall budget and ancestor concurrency ceilings; a static cap does not enforce a shared cap across independent run-input processes. Each selected branch completes before downstream measurement. Direction-specific designers test their finite candidate against the one shared contract; they do not create independent baselines, profiles or oracles.

### Profile-driven direction selection and handoff

For each round, select relevant directions from the current trusted profile, source flow and residual bottlenecks. Record why each direction is needed. Select from sixteen optimization directions and three support roles without filling slots with irrelevant work. Reprofiling remains with the original measurement owner and its actual grants.

Agree the current direction's bounded stage tasks and completion evidence. Once its authorized tasks, matched measurements and original cleanup are complete, or its original stop is retained with unfinished reasons, the supervisor may select the next priority only while original admission remains open. Use actual profile/residual-bottleneck evidence of diminishing benefit, completed priority or a newly dominant direction. A noisy or isolated micro-gain does not establish full convergence. Changing priority neither advances the accepted baseline nor replaces full quality/acceptance gates; preserve every failed, insufficient and unknown result.

A direction switch always changes experts. Hand off to the new direction's different existing owner when available; otherwise admit that direction's new owner through the supported original path. If that owner is busy, queue its task rather than create another owner. The previous expert finishes its current authorized task, preserves its direction's results, checkpoint and original experiment/attempt/job identities, and may idle awaiting more tasks in that same direction. Keep that expert in its original direction; never repurpose it into the next one or interrupt another expert's unrelated work.

Carry the complete original source/configuration/workload, approved plan, accepted baseline and candidate identities, paired results, trusted profiles, quality evidence, remaining budgets/clock/cleanup reserve and queued dependencies into the handoff. Reconcile unknown work only through its original run, without replay. Select any later composition revision before its freeze through original admission; the current frozen DAG and member slots do not change. Waiting, handoff and any authorized reprofile consume the original bounds; no new task ID renews them, and global stop/deadline/exhaustion prevents new admission. Retain required flow, design, kernel assessment and independent review. This static recipe adds planning and handoff instructions, not automatic reprofiling, dynamic dispatch or an optimization loop.

## Specialist execution and supervision

The supervisor only dispatches, coordinates resources, verifies evidence and reports. Keep formal owner approvals. Name the executor, resources, dependencies and completion criteria for each experiment, fix or commit. Keep one retained expert per direction and one direction per expert in the original project, workload or campaign. Queue work for its owner, reuse supported sessions or checkpoints and verify handoff before succession without overlap.

On completion or blocker, record outcome and next step. Immediately check readiness and dispatch authorized ready work. Fill vacant needed directions and advance independent tasks. Aim for 4–8 active experts with distinct assigned directions based on the latest trusted profile and source evidence. Count only owners actively doing assigned work. Report actual count and blocker if fewer can run. Select members and dependencies before freeze, budget calls and separate writes where applicable.

The supervisor keeps a durable task-list file within existing write authority. Update each task's pending, in progress or completed status, executor, related files, blocker, next ready work and original backend/job or run IDs on progress or handoff. An external Job with its own backend lifetime may survive session loss. Resume from the queue and reconcile original state before acting on that work. Uncertainty alone authorizes no resend, reassignment or cancellation. Preserve frozen candidate and members, caps, grants, clock, budgets, STOP and cleanup.

The proposal skeleton and its single-leaf entry can provide fewer than four concurrent specialists. Select real topic descendants before freeze and use the original supported execution owner.

The task's assigned nodes, GPU groups or resource pool and resource/experiment budget provide standing execution authority to the assigned specialist. Within that scope and its actual tool grants, the specialist independently chooses GPU count and topology, starts or submits work, queues experiments, collects and reconciles results, and continues or resumes experiments without waiting for the supervisor's confirmation at each step or run. Use the original execution owner and supported tools for job effects, recovery and cleanup. Keep original experiment/attempt/job identities, the total budget, stop fences and unknown-outcome reconciliation intact when continuing work.

Specialists report allocations, queue/run identities, results, remaining budget and blockers to the supervisor. Readonly proposal leaves supply hypotheses and handoffs under their declared grants. Report unavailable tools and unsupported operations.

### AI helper delegation limit

Each non-supervisor AI agent may create at most one distinct direct helper over its original project/campaign lifetime; zero is valid. Keep original parent/helper IDs. Same-helper turns consume original call/time budgets but no new child slot. Completion/failure/cancellation, retries, new task IDs, renaming or model/backend/tool/subtask aliases never reset or bypass lifetime counting; reconcile unknown creation by its original ID. That helper creates no AI children or relay chains. Further needs return to the original top-level supervisor for original roster/resource/budget admission or queueing; agents cannot self-promote or add graph members. Helpers do not count as direction owners.

This caps AI delegation, including AI workers behind tools, not ordinary OS/rank/DataLoader/non-agent MCP processes or the trusted executor's already-frozen DAG fanout. It adds no spawn grant or runtime counter; report unavailable capability. Original global depth/instance/concurrency/model/time/resource/STOP and cleanup limits remain.

### Review scope and cadence

Check only current-stage/current-goal needs. Routine experts self-check; avoid redundant proof/tests/reviewer passes once evidence is adequate. Optimization protects agreed model/objective/precision, numeric/gradient correctness, actual profile/matched throughput-memory evidence and owned resources/budgets/STOP/cleanup; defer style/directories/abstraction polish unless they affect current correctness/performance/reliability. Development: function/interfaces/failure paths; environment: compatibility/repro/smoke; documentation: claims/links. Required original contracts remain.

Review one coherent ready-to-integrate goal batch at integration/merge, not each file/commit/preparation/env-binding/small fix. Keep related feature preparation/code/tests/fixes and cross-module end-to-end changes together. Split only separately deliverable goals/contracts needing separate integration or a batch that cannot be adequately reviewed; no fixed size threshold. Use the original formal FinalReviewDAG as one batch gate with every required member/stage and no duplicate external same-axis layer. Otherwise use fresh-context independent review of necessary scope, semantic ownership/minimal diff, required tests and candidate evidence. Resolve/recheck with the same reviewer within the batch, then release; no resident or recursive status/follow-up review. Original trusted/numerical/performance/evidence and STOP/cleanup gates remain.

## GPU comparisons and pool validation

Use **2-GPU** comparisons, or **4 GPUs** when needed, as inexpensive starting points when they fit the unchanged workload and exercise the relevant bottleneck. The assigned specialist selects the GPU count and topology appropriate to its resources and experimental question. Keep model algorithm, structure, size, precision and training objective fixed for the first optimization round. Compare the baseline and candidate on the same GPU count, hardware/topology, workload and measurement contract; record throughput, peak memory, correctness and measurement uncertainty.

Accumulate candidates with measured benefit into a batch for larger acceptance runs. The assigned specialist may queue and submit multiple **8-GPU jobs**, or other multi-GPU/node scales that exercise the target behavior within its assigned resources. Each target scale needs its own matched baseline/candidate comparison and original full quality/evidence gate. Small-scale gain promotes a candidate to this queue; it does not certify large-scale gain or advance the accepted baseline. Keep failed, noisy, no-gain and unrepresentative small-scale results visible.

For iGS resource pools, use **igs-cli (`igs`)** with actually available, authenticated and supported operations. Follow the available iGS job/resource skills for real specifications, mounts, images and multi-node environments; all iGS commands use `--format json`. A prepared request is submitted with `igs job create --region REGION --file experiment-job.json --format json`. Preserve actual pool/quota limits, concurrency, wall-time and cleanup budgets. A GPU visibility mask does not change how many GPUs a pool specification reserves.

Retain the exact request and returned job ID, then inspect the created job. A lost or ambiguous create response requires read-only reconciliation, not another submission. Platform creation or Running status does not prove that training or acceptance succeeded. Keep source/recipe/environment identities, GPU count/topology, paired raw results, quality checks, resource cost and the promotion/acceptance decision in the experiment report.

After target-scale acceptance, the assigned execution specialist feeds the verified results and remaining bottleneck into the next comparison round and continues this small-test → candidate-batch → target-scale-acceptance cycle within the original task's bounds.

## Execution boundary

This is a static readonly proposal graph. Dependencies express intended order; each leaf's task binding preserves the original root task. A standalone prepare-input/run-input does not execute predecessors or automatically attach their outputs. The operator must provide the original task/source plus the complete results required above on each call. Never infer that a dependency has run from its presence in the graph.

The trusted experiment runner retains command execution, resource allocation, stop/recovery and evidence; the owner separately authorizes adoption and later integration. Record a proposed next bottleneck and bounded stop/rollback conditions. Automatic branching, repeated optimization, cross-round budget enforcement and baseline advancement are not implemented by this recipe.

Method basis: Ergora-authored orchestration from the pinned Bridge evidence-gated optimization, PyTorch completion/profiler and TorchTitan/slime ownership research documented by the shipped specialist resources. The child Skills carry their complete operative methods and provenance. This root introduces no external framework dependency or runtime integration.
