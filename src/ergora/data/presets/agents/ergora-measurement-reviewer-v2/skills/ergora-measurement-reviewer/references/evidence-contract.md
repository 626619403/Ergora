# Measurement and adoption evidence

Ergora-authored synthesis. These offline rules and citations do not grant external access or substitute for original experiment artifacts.

A proposal can be coherent without having run. Its review can approve proposal completeness only; missing required correctness, quality or performance evidence cannot be promoted to measured success. Keep exact code/config/data/candidate/baseline identities, commands, software/hardware versions, rank/mesh and original stdout/report artifacts. Record whether values were measured, estimated, supplied by an operator or synthetic.

Check objective units, comparable tokens/work, warmup, synchronization/capture, measurement window, repetitions, variance and resource costs. Preserve all-rank peak memory and stragglers rather than averaging away failures. A single best sample, an altered tolerance, a changed baseline or an omitted failed shape is not adequate evidence of the originally requested gain. State statistical limits without inventing confidence intervals or rerunning the experiment.

Correctness/quality, structural integration, test value and performance validity are distinct questions; an independent reviewer can specialize in each. Keep kernel, wrapper and end-to-end claim scopes separate. Combined interventions require the combined candidate's real checks. The original experiment runner and evidence-bound owner gate retain execution, stop/recovery and adoption authority; this review creates none of those records.
